====================================================================================================
6. Decision
====================================================================================================
PIP: PLAIN drum (no reeving). With SCS0009 the travel budget, not the strand, is the limit, and reeving is 16 % worse
per servo degree; with HLS the MCP (120 N strand class) limits the pinch, so reeving the PIP adds only ~9 %.
MCP: reverse fork, fixed Ø4 tube axle, ONE MR84ZZ idler for both pip strands (the two strands' contact speeds
are always equal, so one race is enough). pip FLEX passes on the idler's DORSAL side (wrap grows with flexion, no
redirect, no sliding); pip EXT on the palm side with a Ø3 redirect dowel that pre-wraps it 106 deg (a strand on the
palm side of a coaxial idler otherwise bow-strings off it after ~12 deg of MCP: SYNTHESIS item 6 as written
does not work for the palm-side strand). MR63 needs a stepped axle; MR84 gives 0.585 coupling and still fits.
Spools: SCS MCP r 4 / PIP r 5 / abd r 5; HLS r 5 everywhere.
