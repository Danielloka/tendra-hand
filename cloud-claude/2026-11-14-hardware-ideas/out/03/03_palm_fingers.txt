A. How far can one finger adduct toward a straight neighbour (relative angle)?
   condition at distance z from the pivot: z*sin(th) <= gap(z); gap = pitch - (w_a(z)+w_b(z))/2 (both taper)
   index-middle  untapered                gap at pivot 2.0 mm  -> max relative adduction  1.2 deg (limit at z=95 mm)
   index-middle  taper 18->14             gap at pivot 2.4 mm  -> max relative adduction  3.6 deg (limit at z=95 mm)
   index-middle  taper 18->13             gap at pivot 2.5 mm  -> max relative adduction  4.2 deg (limit at z=95 mm)
   ring-little   untapered (17/15.5)      gap at pivot 1.5 mm  -> max relative adduction  0.9 deg (limit at z=95 mm)
   ring-little   taper 17->13             gap at pivot 2.6 mm  -> max relative adduction  3.3 deg (limit at z=95 mm)
   Tapering the phalanges like a human finger (18 -> 14 mm) buys ~3x more adduction for free (less plastic).
   The ring/little 9 deg limit in V2 is about the staggered YOKES, not the phalanges: yokes need the same taper
   (narrower yoke on the side facing the neighbour) or 2 mm more pitch.
