Live sensor readings

Sensor reads|B| mTNeighbourJointField turn

Computed from the dipole formula for every magnet at its modelled position, plus Earth's field in Norway (~50 µT). "Neighbour" is the field from the other magnets as a share of the paired magnet's field. TMAG5273A1 range: ±40 mT, noise ~10 µT, so aim for 0.3–40 mT. "Field turn" is how far the field vector has rotated since the flat pose. Calibration maps it to the joint angle.
