7. Alternatives ruled out (numbers)
================================================================================================
  Central compression post / ball at the wrist centre: the 32 strands occupy the centre. A ring of eyes around a
  Ø10 post puts every eye at r >= 6.5 mm -> compensation travel 10|x| + 20|y| up to ~190 deg: no SCS servo fits.
  Spherical shell bearing around the bundle (Ø>=40, engaged over +-52 deg): collides with the cardan lugs and
  the push-rod balls inside the 30 mm gap; not buildable at hobby scale.
  Keep sheaths across the wrist (report 09 §7 fallback): the coil housings carry the compression instead (V2 path).
