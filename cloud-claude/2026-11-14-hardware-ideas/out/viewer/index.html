All 32 finger and thumb tendons cross the wrist through the steel eye plate at its centre. Bending the wrist changes each tendon's length in a simple, straight-line way that its servo corrects. The ring, yokes and pins are metal, because the tendons push 0.74 kN (SCS0009) to 1.67 kN (HLS3606M) through the wrist. The two pink pins are hollow and carry the 6 sensor wires.

The whole V2.1 hand

Four fingers with an angle sensor in every joint, touch pads and fingertip sensors; the thumb; a hollow palm with the sensor hub board; the metal wrist; and the forearm with 16 SCS0009 servos and their spool cartridges. About 1.1 kg and 500 parts.

Known clashes still to fix: the ring and little knuckles touch in "thumb to little" and "spread", and some palm tendon liners overlap.

What is new

Bare tendons over steel pins and bearings instead of PTFE tubes. A fixed steel knuckle axle with the sensor wires inside it. Metal rings in the drum grooves. Fuse-cup anchors that break before the servo. A snap-in silicone fingertip.

Sensors: a magnet in the end of every joint pin with a TMAG5273 angle chip facing it (about 0.5° after calibration), ceramic bearings beside them so steel does not bend the field, a 3-axis magnetic touch sensor in the tip, two pressure pads, and one 4-wire bundle that twists through the joint axes to a plug at the finger root.
