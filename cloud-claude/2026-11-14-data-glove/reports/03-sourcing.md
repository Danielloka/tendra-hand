# 03 — Parts, prices and sourcing in Norway (research agent, Sonnet)

Rate ~10.5 NOK/USD + 25 % VAT. "(est.)" = estimate, not a found price.

- TMAG5273 breakouts: Adafruit A2 $5.95; SparkFun Qwiic ~$4.95, Mini $6.50–7.90 (fixed address → mux needed).
- XIAO ESP32-S3 $8.49 (DigiKey). BNO085 $24.95. ICM-42688 breakouts ~$25.
- JLCPCB flex: promo $2 for 5 × 2-layer ≤ 100 × 100 mm, 4–6 days, plus shipping/VAT.
- Cameras: RealSense D405 ~$276–550, often backordered, min range ~7 cm (best close-up); OAK-D SR $199–329; Gemini 335L $359–449 (25 cm min); a cheap USB RGB camera is the budget option.
- AS5600 ~40 NOK, MT6701 ~50, TCA9548A 20–70, magnets 10–60 NOK per pack, LiPo 40–70, glove 100–250 NOK (est.).

Option A (magnetometer glove, JLC assembly) ≈ 1,700–2,600 NOK; option B (encoder exoskeleton) ≈ 1,100–1,700 NOK. Either + D405 exceeds 4,000 NOK, so start with a cheap camera or a used D405.
