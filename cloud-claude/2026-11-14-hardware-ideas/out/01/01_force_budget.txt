====================================================================================================
4d. BALANCED designs: own spool per joint (MCP = smallest metal hub, plain drum; PIP = 2:1 reeved drum)
====================================================================================================
Why: the MCP drum has the pip strands passing through its middle, so a 2-groove reeved MCP does not fit in the
8.8 mm between the MCP bearings. The PIP drum has room (7 mm). Each servo has its own spool, so each joint gets
its own ratio. In an antagonistic loop the ratio is capped by the EXTENSOR path (its spool radius >= hub minimum),
so reeving must double both strands (shared 2-groove track, as below).

A joint-angle sensor (magnet + Hall) at MCP/PIP lets the ESP32 servo the JOINT, and the sag becomes a force sensor:
